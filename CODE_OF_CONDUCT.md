# Code of Conduct

Be kind and respectful. Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination are not welcome in issues,
pull requests or any other project space. Assume good faith, keep feedback about the work, and
remember that many contributors are beginners at Go, Japanese, or both.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments and block contributors who do not follow this. Report
problems privately through the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
